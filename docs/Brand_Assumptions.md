# Brand assumptions and factual boundaries

XUYU / 序隅 is an entirely fictional luxury leather-goods brand. Its four catalog products, all clients, transactions and boutique/event settings are invented test fixtures. The tool helps a Client Advisor draft WeChat messages; it sends nothing and does not learn new client facts automatically from model output.

The intended relationship is attentive, individual and discreet. Latest explicit instructions take priority over older preferences. New clients receive the same care without invented intimacy. Gender, recorded age and occupation are sourced background, never proxies for taste, wealth, personality or purchase intent. Long-term records are manually confirmed, scoped, timestamped and revision checked.

Only the versioned catalog establishes product facts, prices, material, colors and features. Stock, delivery, weight, laptop fit, production origin, handmade processes, scarcity, useful life, resale value and repair eligibility remain unknown unless explicitly provided. Advisors can explain the listed price and seek verification; they cannot promise discounts, price matching, gifts, free repairs or refunds. Event details require supplied confirmation. Cultural quotations require supplied verified text and attribution; otherwise use original imagery.

Client-facing language is affirmative. Describe actual conditions and verification steps positively while directly answering the question. “不凡” and “不同” are not denials by themselves. Automated phrase checks are incomplete; semantic review is separate. Value connects one verified detail to the client’s stated use, taste or personal milestone. Price-only requests receive price and terms, ending there.

All scores are project-defined editorial judgments. Emotional value has not been tested on real customers, and conversion improvement has not been measured. The participating implementation assistant is the AI evaluator; human adoption labels and optional scores remain separate.
